FAQ

How do I get my purchases back on a new phone?
Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

How do I remove ads?
With the one-time "Remove ads" purchase, the Starter pack or VIP. Rewarded ads (continue or x2 coins) are always optional.

How do I cancel VIP?
iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I lost my progress.
Progress is saved on your phone. If you uninstall the app or switch phones, it is lost. Purchases can always be recovered with "Restore purchases".

How do I change my ad choices?
In Europe, go to Shop > Ad choices. On iPhone, also Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

I want a refund.
Payments are handled by Apple and Google. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: from your Google Play order history.
