Tube Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

In short:

• No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

• Your progress (scores, coins, skins and settings) is stored only on your phone.

• Ads are served by Google AdMob and purchases are validated by RevenueCat. They process some technical data, as explained below.

• On iPhone, the advertising ID is only used if you allow it. In Europe we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

1. Who we are

Tube Rush is developed and published by Leonardo Elias Natal (Natal Games). For any privacy question, email us at natalgames.contacto@gmail.com.

2. What data is processed and why

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or any other content on your phone. We do not sell your data.

3. Companies that process data

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.): advertising. How Google uses data · Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: purchase management and validation. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play): app downloads and payments, under their own policies.

These companies may process data in the United States or other countries, using the safeguards required by law, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Legal basis (if you live in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland)

  • Saving your progress and validating purchases: necessary to provide the service you request (performance of a contract).
  • Personalized ads and use of the advertising ID: your consent, requested through Google's consent form the first time it is needed.
  • Non-personalized ads, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game.

5. Tracking on iPhone (App Tracking Transparency)

On iPhone, the app asks whether you allow tracking. If you say no, the advertising ID is not shared and ads are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. How to change your choices

  • Ad consent (Europe): in the game, Shop > Ad choices.
  • iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads, where you can delete or reset your Advertising ID.
  • Removing ads: buy "Remove ads" or VIP.

7. How long data is kept

  • Game progress stays on your phone until you uninstall the app.
  • Google keeps advertising data for the periods stated in its policy.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase history as long as needed to restore purchases and meet legal obligations.

8. Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, object to its processing, request portability or withdraw consent by emailing natalgames.contacto@gmail.com. To erase all game progress, simply uninstall the app. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Tube Rush is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Communications with Google and RevenueCat are encrypted. Since we run no servers and have no user accounts, we do not store your data anywhere of our own.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with its date. For important changes we will also let you know inside the game.
